Typical Market Benchmarks

Across similar local MSPs and IT support providers in the U.S., common pricing is roughly:

  • Managed endpoint support: $50-$110/device/month
  • Helpdesk and patch management: $30-$80/user/month
  • Network and firewall management: $100-$220/hr
  • Managed IT for small businesses: $60–$120/device/month

These rates keep the offer competitive while reflecting the value of local support, fast response, and ongoing care.
